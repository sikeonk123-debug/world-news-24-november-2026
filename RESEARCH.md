## Heroes

1. **Cikande fire toll rises to 26 — ASIA / INDONESIA — Xinhua, NYT.**
   The death toll from the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy
   factory in Cikande, Banten, rose to 26 on Tuesday, authorities said,
   as search and rescue continued for workers still missing; 26 body
   bags recovered, DNA identification under way. The fire broke out on
   Saturday evening; the 23 Nov figure was 24 dead. Tag CONFIRMED
   (Xinhua + NYT). Banner: 26 DEAD, DOZENS MISSING.

2. **CAS restores Senegal's AFCON title — AFRICA / SPORT — AP, CAS.**
   The Court of Arbitration for Sport ruled unanimously on Tuesday
   that Senegal's 1–0 win over Morocco in the Africa Cup of Nations
   final stands, cancelling CAF's award of a 3–0 forfeit win to
   Morocco. Morocco plans to appeal to Switzerland's supreme court.
   Tag OFFICIAL (CAS ruling). Banner: SENEGAL CHAMPIONS AGAIN.

3. **Dow tops 50,000 for first time — AMERICAS / MARKETS — Reuters.**
   The Dow closed up 0.85% at 50,018.70 on Tuesday, its first close
   above 50,000, while the S&P 500 fell 0.50% to 7,201.09 and the
   Nasdaq fell 1.48% to 23,733.54 as chip stocks slid (PHLX
   Semiconductor −7.88%). US consumer confidence jumped to 103.3
   (Conference Board). Tag OFFICIAL (market closes). Data-board hero:
   DOW 50,019 +0.85% · S&P 500 7,201 −0.50% · NASDAQ 23,734 −1.48% ·
   CHIPS PHLX −7.88%.
